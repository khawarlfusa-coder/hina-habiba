About Hina Habiba

A respected voice in the realm of Naat Khawani, touching hearts through melodious eulogies in honor of the Holy Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

Biography & Journey

A Soulful Devotion to Praising the Beloved Prophet ﷺ

Hina Habiba is an accomplished female Naat Khawan (Sana Khawan) revered for her emotional depth, classical vocal clarity, and traditional Islamic demeanor. Through her spiritual recitations, she continues to inspire listeners across Pakistan and internationally.

Her repertoire encompasses heartfelt Urdu Naats, rich Punjabi kalams, poignant Manqabats dedicated to the Ahle Bait and Awliya-e-Kiram, and classic Arabic nasheeds. With her dedicated platform HH Production (featured under @HinaProduction-o6e on YouTube), she regularly shares fresh collaborations and timeless spiritual tributes.
